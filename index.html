Benefits and Disadvantages

Good

  • Is able to access localhost servers.
  • Clean look.
  • Less Cookies being transferred.
  • No need to clear your cache.

Bad

  • Website access is limited due to CORS restrictions because it accesses websites using iframes. Websites that use CORS should be accessed through uncors.vercel.app.
  • Specific hostnames are inaccessible.
  • Searching information with the browser too much will result in Google becoming suspicious about your activity, and we highly recommend just accessing URLs.
  • Basic functionality compared to most browsers.
